Introduction

Jaylub (Jakub "Karel" Jaros-Finch), born in late 2009 in the small fictional township of Rockton, resides in the quiet village of Kolesovia.
He is local lore for his legendary refusal to adopt modern hygiene products, having sworn off showers and deodorants around the age of ten. His personal interests include playing amateur football for the regional club Chrastwood FC—where local legend Miloš "The Curve" Jelínek once played—and maintaining an absolute devotion to legacy sandbox games, strictly adhering to release version 1.8.9. He completed primary school in Kolesovia, where his nickname "Jaylub" was first coined by classmates in reference to his unusual eccentricities and habits.
In his tight-knit social circle, he is joined by Jäger (Jan Bauler), his primary companion and the only self-proclaimed "normal" resident willing to brave his presence. Jaylub currently attends a secondary vocational school in Podbor, where former classmates humorously debate whether his reputation followed him.

Averadge Day

He typically awakens around 8:00 AM and heads directly outdoors without any morning routine. He habitually remains in whatever clothes he wore the previous evening, occasionally keeping the same outfit in rotation for months at a time.
At approximately 12:00 PM, his mother calls him inside for breakfast, after which he immediately returns outside to practice football or ride his bicycle. In the late afternoon, he occasionally walks his dog around the estate grounds.
Around 4:00 PM, he returns home for lunch. By the time he ventures back out, dusk is falling, and few peers remain outside. Consequently, Jaylub spends his evenings in the garden practicing ball control. Between 7:00 PM and 8:00 PM, he retreats indoors to his computer setup to play his preferred 1.8.9 game build. In earlier years, he occasionally joined voice channels to chat with former gaming acquaintances while playing.
At 10:00 PM, he is called for dinner. Following the meal, he retires for the night, continuing his routines unchanged.

The Household

The household consists of his mother, his father, Jaylub himself, and two sisters.
His father works as a correctional officer and maintains a standard routine. His mother handles household cooking, though family acquaintances describe her as operating on a very relaxed, unconventional schedule.
His elder sister lives away from the primary household, having previously worked at a cinema multiplex in Zlicin-West. His younger sister is described as highly introverted, primarily interacting only with Jaylub. Former classmates recall humorous primary school anecdotes regarding her playful, eccentric antics during physical education classes and game sessions in the school locker rooms.

The Lore & Anecdotes

The Residence: Visitors describe the family property as an old rural homestead guarded by a heavy stone-gated entrance. The yard features free-roaming poultry, workshops, and traditional outbuildings. The two-story house features a main workshop area, a functional second-floor living space, and a dedicated gaming room.
The Gaming Station: Jaylub’s setup consists of a vintage laptop, an auxiliary monitor, and entry-level peripherals resting atop a heavily utilitarian desk setup.
Pitch Strategy: Opposing players in local amateur football matches frequently comment on Jaylub's formidable defensive play style, noting that his imposing aura and unconventional pitch presence give his team a distinct psychological advantage.
